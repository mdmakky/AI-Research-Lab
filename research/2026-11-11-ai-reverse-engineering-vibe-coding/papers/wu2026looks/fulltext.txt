Figure 2: MobileForge pipeline. (1) Dataset construction starts from three design principles: realism, project
granularity, and runnable evaluation. It collects 29 in-market apps across 14 category buckets with coherent
multi-page screenshot sets (309 screens, mean 10.7 per app). (2) Annotation runs a VLM auto-draft over the
screenshots, and then a human reviewer edits the page-relationship description and the navigation test specifications.

pre-scaffolded directory, verify-repair loop). (4) Evaluation scores the resulting project on five axes, namely build,
navigation, visual fidelity, code maintainability, and efficiency, using state-isolated navigation testing (§4.2) and an
anchor-reference list-wise visual judge (§4.3).
